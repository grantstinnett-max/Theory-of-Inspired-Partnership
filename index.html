How our energy turns into finished projects, and what changes when we hand the work we're not built for to partners who are.

Every project needs two kinds of work

  • Create & connect. Design, creative solutions, content and marketing, bringing people together. Our zone: about 3 back for every 1 we put in.
  • Build & manage, done by us. Construction, management, and the rest. Not our zone: we get back less than we put in.
  • Build & manage, done by partners. People who love that side of the work, getting their own 3 : 1.

How the numbers work

  1. Every project is about 20 parts create & connect and 80 parts build & manage.
  2. In our zone, each unit of our energy produces about three. Outside it, we get back less than we put in.
  3. Say that doing it all ourselves, one project takes all of our energy for a year. We're fast at the 20 creative parts, so they use only about 7% of it. The other 93% goes into the 80 parts we're slow at, returning about 0.86 for every 1.
  4. With all of our energy in our zone, 100 becomes 300 parts of create & connect: enough for 15 projects at 20 parts each.
  5. Partners carry the 80 build & manage parts of each project in their own zone, at about 3 : 1. Total effort per project drops to about a third, so roughly 4 months instead of 12.

Not to scale. The numbers illustrate the idea; they aren't a forecast.
